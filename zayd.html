His Story

Zayd's Journey

Zayd has Farber disease — a condition caused by mutations in the ASAH1 gene that lead to a deficiency of acid ceramidase. When this enzyme is absent or insufficient, ceramide accumulates in the body, triggering inflammation and progressive damage to the joints, nerves, and organs. It is the same underlying disease spectrum as SMA-PME, and it is just as rare, just as serious, and just as deserving of the research investment it has long lacked.

Zayd's family chose to share their experience publicly — a courageous act that brought Farber disease into the spotlight when their story was covered by KENS 5 news in October 2025. In doing so, they gave a face and a voice to a condition that most people have never heard of, and helped build the awareness that drives research funding forward.

"Their hope — and ours — is that the research will find the breakthrough that changes everything for children like Zayd."

Zayd is one of ten children in our global community living with acid ceramidase deficiency disorders. Each one represents not just a family's fight, but a data point in a rare disease landscape where every patient matters. The research being pursued through this community is not abstract — it is aimed directly at the biology that affects Zayd and every child like him.

Zayd's full story is coming soon. In the meantime, his presence in this community is a reminder of what is at stake — and why this research is a race against time.
